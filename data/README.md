# Data

All lattices are tori with unit spacing. `L` is the linear size, `dimension` the spatial
dimension n and `N = L^n` the number of vertices. `A` is the self-potential and `S` the norm of
the potential, as defined in the paper; for a marked set they are `A_M` and `S_M`. Logarithms
are natural. Which file supports which result of the paper is listed in the
[main README](../README.md#contents).

## Single marked vertex

**`spectral.csv`** (from `src/torus.rs`). `A` from direct spectral sums, for n = 1, 2, 3 and,
for n = 2, up to L = 8192 (Fig. 1c).

**`lattice_constants.csv`** (from `python/make_data.py`). `A` and `S` from the Fourier transform
of the inverse Laplacian (Table S2). This evaluation of `A` is independent of `spectral.csv` and
agrees with it to all printed digits.

**`tulsi_trajectory.csv`** (from `python/make_data.py`). Tulsi's walk on the L × L torus,
L = 16, …, 1024, one row per local step `t`.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `F_over_A` | weighted overlap after local step t, divided by A |
| `p` | probability of finding the walker at the marked vertex |
| `rate` | \|ΔF\| in step t, in units of the sharpened speed limit sqrt(A/2N) |

**`tulsi_summary.csv`** (from `python/make_data.py`). Table S3 and Fig. 2c. One row per L,
evaluated at the step `T` of largest success probability within the first ceil(1.5 π L sqrt(A))
steps.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `T`, `p`, `F_T_over_A` | peak step, success probability and weighted overlap at the peak |
| `max_rate`, `mean_rate` | largest and mean \|ΔF\| per local step up to T, in units of sqrt(A/2N) |
| `drop` | A − F_T divided by the decrease A(1 − sqrt(1−p) − 1/L) − S/L that the bound requires |
| `T_theorem`, `T_min` | lower bound of the Theorem and the sharpened bound, at the same p |
| `T_over_T_min`, `T_over_LsqrtlnL` | T / T_min and T / (L sqrt(ln L)) |

**`tulsi_independent.csv`** (from `src/torus.rs`). A second, independently written simulation of
the same walk for L ≤ 256: `peak_step` and `peak_probability` agree with `T` and `p` above;
`norm_error` is the largest deviation of the norm from one at the sampled steps.

## Marked sets

Generated by `src/marked_equilibrium.rs` for L = 64, 128, 256, 512, 1024 and k = 1, 4, 16, 64,
256 on the L × L torus. With ℓ = L / sqrt(k), the patterns are

| `pattern` | Marked set | Samples |
|---|---|---|
| `periodic` | subgrid of spacing ℓ | 1 |
| `cluster` | compact sqrt(k) × sqrt(k) square | 1 |
| `jittered` | one uniformly random vertex in each ℓ × ℓ window | 4 (2 at L = 1024) |
| `random` | uniformly random k-subset | 8 (4 at L = 1024) |

For k = 1 only `periodic` is listed. A sample is identified by `(pattern, L, k, rep)`; its
random seed is `20261001 + 10000 L + 20 k + rep`. Fig. 2a quotes `A` for the `periodic` and
`cluster` rows with L = 64, k = 16; Fig. 2b plots L = 1024 (medians over samples); Table S4 is
`marked_summary.csv` at L = 1024, k = 64; the sharpened bound 55.61 in its caption is
`periodic_sharp_p_two_thirds` of the `periodic` row of `marked_equilibrium.csv`.

**`marked_patterns.csv`**. Coordinates `x`, `y` of the marked vertices of every sample.

**`marked_equilibrium.csv`**. One row per sample (261 rows).

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `A`, `S2`, `eta` | A_M, S_M² and the endpoint ratio S_M / (sqrt(N) A_M) |
| `scaled_A_4pi_k_over_log_Nk` | 4π k A_M / ln(N/k) |
| `uniform_energy`, `uniform_over_equilibrium` | energy of the uniform charge on the set, and its ratio to A_M |
| `rho_min`, `rho_max` | smallest and largest equilibrium charge on a marked vertex |
| `t_hit`, `sqrt_t_hit` | mean hitting time 4 N A_M from the uniform distribution, and its square root |
| `lower_p_two_thirds`, `lower_p_one` | lower bound of Eq. (9) at p = 2/3 and p = 1, set to zero when negative |
| `periodic_sharp_p_two_thirds` | sharpened bound at p = 2/3 (periodic subgrids; zero otherwise) |
| `plateau_error`, `mean_error`, `poisson_error`, `energy_error` | residuals of the constant value on the set, the zero mean, the Poisson equation and the energy identity |
| `periodic_A_error`, `periodic_S2_error` | deviation from the quotient-torus formulas (periodic subgrids; zero otherwise) |
| `one_window_fraction` | fraction of ℓ × ℓ windows, averaged over translations, that contain exactly one marked vertex |

**`marked_summary.csv`**. One row per `(L, k, pattern)`, containing the sample count (`replicates`),
selected medians, `A_min`, `A_max`, `eta_max`, and `positive_two_thirds_count`, the number of samples
with a positive bound at p = 2/3. The bound is evaluated for each sample before the median is taken.
